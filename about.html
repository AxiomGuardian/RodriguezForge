Who We Are

One name. One standard.

Rodriguez Forge is a multi-generational residential construction company rooted in Eloy, Arizona. What began with one man’s hands and reputation has grown into a focused family operation built on craft, accountability, and the belief that a home should be built to last.

Founder & Craftsmanship

Albert Rodriguez

Albert has spent more than 50 years building homes across Eloy and the surrounding desert. His standards were shaped the hard way: through foundations, framing, and decades of work that still stands. He remains the foundation of the company: the license, the reputation, and the non-negotiable expectation that every job carries the Rodriguez name with pride.
